# Bridge Recipes — Marketing Brief (seed for the Marketing project)

_Do not market anything that is not shipped. Never claim features the code lacks._

## Who it is for
1. **Salesforce admins / RevOps** at Shopify brands who want a few useful signals in the CRM, not a data mirror.
2. **Shopify developers and agencies** who keep rebuilding the same Shopify→Salesforce glue.

## The one-line promise
"Three useful Shopify signals in your Salesforce — Leads from corporate buyers, VIP alerts — with a safe preview and no per-task fees."

## Positioning
- Opinionated recipes, not a universal sync.
- **Dry-run first**: see exactly what would be created before anything is written.
- Open source and self-hostable; honest about data (minimal, redacted, consent-aware).

## Claim guardrails
- Do not say "real-time" unless measured; say "within minutes" only with data.
- Do not imply endorsement by Shopify or Salesforce; use "works with".
- Be explicit that sales outreach consent is the merchant's responsibility.

## Proof assets to create
- A 90-second video: place a corporate-domain test order → dry-run log → enable → Lead appears in Salesforce.
- A "what gets written, field by field" diagram.
- A write-up: why recipes beat universal sync.

## Offer ladder (links to `../MONEY_GUIDE.md`)
Free self-host → paid setup package → fixed-price custom recipe. Keep client-specific work private.

## Channels
GitHub README/Discussions · Salesforce Trailblazer community · Shopify Community / Partners forums · LinkedIn technical write-ups · Upwork profile (portfolio proof).
