## 1. What this is
A small, reliable Shopify→Salesforce core plus narrow "recipes" (R1 Retail-to-B2B Lead Spotter, R2 VIP Threshold Alert, R3 Closed-Won → Draft Order). Open source (MIT). This is the reframe of the planned "Universal Shopify ↔ Salesforce Connector": instead of one big sync engine, a growing collection of opinionated recipes on a tiny core. See `README.md`, `docs/SPEC.md`.

## 2. Why this shape (honest read)
- A generic sync engine is a crowded, support-heavy category (enterprise iPaaS, per-task tools, paid syncs). Narrow recipes are shippable, reviewable and individually useful.
- It is the strategic bridge for the whole portfolio: once the core exists, other cross-platform ideas (e.g. practitioner revenue rollup from 04) are new recipes.
- Commercial link: the same core can be sold as a fixed-price service ("Shopify orders → Salesforce Leads/Tasks in one week", see `../MONEY_GUIDE.md`). Client work can fund building the core the owner wanted anyway — keep anything client-specific private and push generic improvements upstream.
- **Weakest point:** R1 touches protected customer data (name/email) and CRM privacy law, so publishing widely is the riskiest part — hence self-host first.
